## Third-party art (demo only, not part of the addon)

All files are unmodified copies from Kenney packs, licence
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (credit
"Kenney (www.kenney.nl)" given voluntarily). Downloaded 2026-10-18 from the
official pack pages; archive SHA-256: Tiny Dungeon
`c109438ab06f65fd80f9b2686a4cf9c7c11dc64444b47333ec71d602f8bb5fc7`, Tiny Town
`9768692dccff1d706408a5aedd6ca4f6cd1409506cbc84cb2f862919764be977`. The pack
licence files are kept next to the art.

The demo world (`demo/world/`) draws from the packed sheets at run time, no
extra files: Tiny Town tiles 0–2 (grass floor) and the Tiny Dungeon knight tile
(`tile_0084`, region 0,112 16×16) as the player; item icons come from
`demo/art/items/`. The player's light is a generated gradient, not an image.
